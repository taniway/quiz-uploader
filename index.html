Taniway uploader

An internal tool that uploads and schedules videos made by Taniway on Taniway's own YouTube channels.

What it does

  • Uploads finished videos (long videos and Shorts) that Taniway produced itself.
  • Sets each video's title, description, tags, thumbnail, subtitles and publish time.
  • Works only on YouTube channels owned by Taniway: Chess Autopilot and Tick & Trivia, and future Taniway channels. Each channel is connected separately by its owner.

Who uses it

Only Taniway, from its own computer. The tool is not offered to the public, has no other users and does not access anyone else's YouTube account or data.

YouTube API Services

Taniway uploader uses YouTube API Services. Use of the tool is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
